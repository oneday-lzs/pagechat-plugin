Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

PageChat is a Chrome extension. We do not require an account and we do not sell personal data.

Data stored on your device

Chats, settings (temperature, crawl limits, page-mode default), and pending right-click actions are stored in Chrome’s extension storage on your computer. You can export a chat as Markdown or clear all chats from the side panel.

When page content is sent

PageChat does not send the current tab by default. Text leaves your browser only when you take an action that needs it:

  • You check Include this page and send a message
  • You use Summarize site or Company info (public same-site pages are fetched)
  • You use a context-menu action such as ask, translate, or summarize on a page or selection

In those cases, the prompt plus the relevant page or site extract is sent to the PageChat proxy at https://pagechat-plugin.vercel.app/api/chat, then to the hosted model provider to generate a reply. The model API key stays on the server and is not stored in the extension.

What we do not collect

We do not create user accounts, analytics profiles, or advertising IDs. We do not sell or rent the content of your chats. Server logs may include standard request metadata (time, status code) used to keep the proxy running.

Permissions

  • sidePanel — open the chat panel
  • storage — save chats and settings on your device
  • activeTab, tabs, scripting — read the current page when you ask PageChat to
  • contextMenus — right-click actions
  • Host access to websites — fetch public pages you choose to summarize
  • Host access to this site — send chat requests to the hosted model proxy
